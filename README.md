## 💡 How It Works

The software performs several tasks to turn your video into text.

### Video Retrieval
The app connects to the platform link you provide. It fetches the video file or audio track directly from the source. It supports major platforms including Bilibili, YouTube, Douyin, and Kuaishou.

### Speech Transcription
Once the app has the audio, it uses a process called speech-to-text. This creates a full transcript of everything said in the video. The accuracy depends on the quality of the original audio.

### Artificial Intelligence Analysis
After the app creates the transcript, it sends the text to an AI model. The model identifies key themes, summaries, and important details. It organizes this information into a clean Markdown document.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, try these steps:

* **App fails to start:** Right-click the shortcut and select "Run as administrator."
* **Download error:** Check your internet connection. Some videos have restrictions that prevent downloads.
* **Note is empty:** Ensure the video has a clear audio track. If there is no speech, the app cannot generate a transcript.
* **Update required:** Check the release page periodically to ensure you use the latest version with new features.
